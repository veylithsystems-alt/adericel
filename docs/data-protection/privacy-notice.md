# Privacy notice

For people whose personal data Veylith Systems Ltd holds as a controller: users
of Adericel, people invited to it, people who begin a signup, and business
contacts Veylith approaches.

**If your employer uses Adericel and you are asking about data Adericel observed
in your employer's systems** — your account, your laptop, your mailbox — Veylith
is not the controller of that data; your employer is. Ask them, and they can ask
Veylith for help answering. This notice does not cover it.

## Who we are

Veylith Systems Ltd, registered in England and Wales. Contact:
`privacy@veylith.com`.

## What we hold and why

**If you have an Adericel login:** your name, work email address, a hash of your
password, your second-factor secrets, and the addresses and browsers your
sessions came from. We hold it to let you sign in and to record what you
decided. The basis is our contract with you or your employer.

**If you were invited but never accepted:** your work email address, for 90 days
past the invitation's expiry, then deleted.

**If you began a signup and never finished:** your name, work email and the
address you signed up from, for 90 days, then deleted.

**If we approached you about Adericel:** your name, work email and company. Our
basis is legitimate interests — reaching a named role at a business about a
product relevant to it. You can object at any time, and we delete rather than
flag.

## What we do with it

We authenticate you, we attribute the decisions you make, we investigate
security incidents, and we contact you about the service. That is the whole list.

**We do not**: sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train any model,
profile you, or make automated decisions about you.

## How long

The full schedule is at `docs/data-protection/retention.md`. In short: account
data while your account is active; session and second-factor addresses for 90
days; the record of decisions you made for seven years, because that record
belongs to the organisation whose systems were changed and is what they would
need if a decision were ever disputed.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold, to correct it, to delete it, to
restrict what we do with it, to object, and to have it sent somewhere else. We
answer within one month.

**One limit, stated plainly.** If you delete your account, we delete your
credentials, your sessions, your second factors and your outstanding invitations,
and we replace your name with a pseudonym everywhere it appears. But we keep the
record of approvals you gave to change an organisation's systems, with your name
removed. That record is theirs, not ours and not yours: destroying it would take
away their ability to show who authorised a change to their infrastructure. We
rely on Article 17(3)(e) — establishing, exercising and defending legal claims.
We will tell you this rather than quietly doing it, and you can complain about it.

## Who else sees it

The current list is at `docs/data-protection/subprocessors.md`. It is short:
the hosting provider, the payment provider if the deployment takes card
payments, and the email service that delivers messages you asked for. No
analytics vendor, no session replay, no advertising network.

**Veylith's own staff cannot read customer data as a matter of course.** Our
administrators hold no standing access to any customer's assurance data. Getting
access requires issuing a time-limited grant that appears in the audit trail
with a name against it. That is enforced in the software, not promised in a
policy.

## Complaining

Tell us first: `privacy@veylith.com`. If we do not put it right, you can complain
to the Information Commissioner's Office at `ico.org.uk`, or to your local
supervisory authority.

## Changes

We will tell you before this notice changes in a way that affects you, rather
than changing it quietly and dating it.
